# Win/Loss Report: Iberia Grid

Customer: Iberia Grid
Region: Southern Europe
Date: 2025-09-12
Outcome: Won
Our price: EUR 6,100,000
Competitor: Altrix
Competitor price: EUR 6,000,000

## Summary

Iberia Grid tendered converter transformers for a 500 MW interconnector. The customer evaluated the total cost of ownership over 30 years, including electrical losses.

## Why we won

Our design offered guaranteed no-load losses that were 12 percent lower than the Altrix design. Over 30 years the loss savings were worth more than the EUR 100,000 price difference between our bid of EUR 6,100,000 and the Altrix bid of EUR 6,000,000.

## Lessons

Evaluating total cost of ownership favours our low-loss designs. We should ask customers early whether losses are capitalised in the evaluation.
