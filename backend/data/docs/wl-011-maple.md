# Win/Loss Report: Maple Hydro

On 8 April 2026 Maple Hydro, a hydropower operator in North America, informed us that it had awarded the contract for generator step-up transformers to GridCore. We lost this deal.

We quoted EUR 8.2 million. GridCore quoted EUR 7.3 million, which is almost 11 percent lower than our offer. Maple Hydro told us that the technical proposals were comparable and that the decision was made on price alone.

GridCore manufactures in North America, which removed import duties and shipping costs from their quote. Our offer had to include both because the units would have been built in Europe.

Next steps: sales should check whether a North American manufacturing partner could make us competitive in this region.
