# Win/Loss Report: Costa Verde Electric

Customer: Costa Verde Electric
Region: Southern Europe
Date: 2025-07-30
Outcome: Lost
Our price: EUR 3,300,000
Competitor: VoltEdge
Competitor price: EUR 3,350,000

## Summary

Costa Verde Electric requested a STATCOM for the grid connection of a new onshore wind farm near Valencia. The scope included commissioning and a five-year service agreement.

## Why we lost

We were cheaper than VoltEdge by EUR 50,000, so price was not the reason. The customer chose VoltEdge because it has a service team based in Valencia with a 24 hour response time. Our nearest service team is more than 72 hours away.

## Lessons

For wind farm connections, local service presence is as important as price. We should name a local service partner in the offer.
