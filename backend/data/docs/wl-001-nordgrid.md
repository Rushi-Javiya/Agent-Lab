# Win/Loss Report: Nordgrid Utilities

Customer: Nordgrid Utilities
Region: Northern Europe
Date: 2025-02-14
Outcome: Lost
Our price: EUR 4,200,000
Competitor: VoltEdge
Competitor price: EUR 3,850,000

## Summary

Nordgrid Utilities invited bids for a STATCOM of plus or minus 100 MVAr to stabilise the voltage at a substation upgrade in Norway. Four suppliers submitted offers. Our technical solution was rated equal to the winner by the customer's evaluation team.

## Why we lost

Price was the deciding factor. VoltEdge offered EUR 3,850,000, which is about 8 percent below our bid of EUR 4,200,000. The customer confirmed that no technical requirement separated the two offers.

## Lessons

We should prepare a modular price option for STATCOM tenders, so that optional monitoring features can be removed to close a price gap.
