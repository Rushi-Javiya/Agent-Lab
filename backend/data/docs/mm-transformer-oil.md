# Maintenance Manual: Transformer Oil

## Sampling interval

Take an oil sample from every power transformer every 12 months for dissolved gas analysis. Increase the frequency to every 6 months if any gas value exceeds the attention threshold in the last report.

## Limits

The moisture content of the oil must stay below 15 ppm for transformers above 100 kV. The breakdown voltage of the oil must stay above 50 kV. Recondition or replace the oil when either limit is exceeded.

## Records

Keep all oil reports for the lifetime of the transformer. Attach each report to the asset record in the maintenance system.
