# Win/Loss Report: Baltic Power Transmission

Customer: Baltic Power Transmission
Region: Northern Europe
Date: 2025-03-28
Outcome: Won
Our price: EUR 2,750,000
Competitor: GridCore
Competitor price: EUR 2,900,000

## Summary

Baltic Power Transmission tendered two 145 kV gas-insulated switchgear bays for a coastal substation. The site is close to a wind farm, so harmonic distortion was a key concern for the customer.

## Why we won

The decisive factor was our harmonic filter design, which kept the total harmonic distortion below 1.5 percent in the customer's simulation. GridCore did not provide a comparable simulation. Our price of EUR 2,750,000 was also lower than the GridCore offer of EUR 2,900,000.

## Lessons

Early simulation results in the bid package convinced the technical committee. We will include a simulation summary in every switchgear bid.
