# Win/Loss Report: Ostsee Energie

Ostsee Energie, a utility in Northern Europe, awarded us a contract on 27 February 2026 for four 110 kV power transformers. We won the deal against Altrix.

Our price was EUR 4.85 million and the Altrix price was EUR 4.7 million, so we were more expensive by EUR 150,000. The customer still chose us for technical reasons.

The deciding point was our digital condition monitoring package, which streams oil temperature, gas content and load data to the customer's control centre. Altrix could only offer manual inspections. Ostsee Energie expects to avoid at least one unplanned outage per year with the monitoring data.

Next steps: we will use the Ostsee Energie case as a reference for other utilities that want predictive maintenance.
