# Win/Loss Report: Alpen Energie

Customer: Alpen Energie
Region: Central Europe
Date: 2025-06-21
Outcome: Lost
Our price: EUR 5,600,000
Competitor: NordSwitch
Competitor price: EUR 5,100,000

## Summary

Alpen Energie tendered six 220 kV gas-insulated switchgear bays for a pumped-storage plant in Austria. The tender was run as a pure price competition after a technical pre-qualification that all three bidders passed.

## Why we lost

NordSwitch bid EUR 5,100,000 against our EUR 5,600,000, a difference of EUR 500,000. After pre-qualification the customer ranked the offers by price only.

## Lessons

When a tender is price-only after pre-qualification, we need approval for a lower margin before the bid is submitted. Waiting for management approval cost us time.
