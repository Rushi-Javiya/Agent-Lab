# Maintenance Manual: STATCOM Cooling System

## Coolant

Replace the deionised water coolant every 5 years. The conductivity of the coolant must stay below 2 microsiemens per centimetre. Measure the conductivity every quarter and record the value.

## Pumps

Replace the pump bearings after 40,000 operating hours. If the cooling pump vibration exceeds the alarm limit, switch to the redundant pump and inspect the main pump within 7 days.

## Heat exchangers

Clean the outdoor heat exchangers once per year. In dusty environments clean them every 6 months.
