# Win/Loss Report: Rheinland Netze

Customer: Rheinland Netze
Region: Central Europe
Date: 2025-05-09
Outcome: Won
Our price: EUR 1,980,000
Competitor: Altrix
Competitor price: EUR 1,800,000

## Summary

Rheinland Netze needed a 63 MVA power transformer to replace a unit that failed unexpectedly in the distribution grid. The customer was under time pressure because one feeder was running without redundancy.

## Why we won

Delivery time decided the tender. We committed to delivery within 14 weeks from a reserved production slot, while Altrix quoted 26 weeks. The customer accepted a price that was EUR 180,000 higher than the Altrix offer of EUR 1,800,000 to restore redundancy sooner.

## Lessons

Reserved production slots are a strong sales argument for emergency replacements. Sales should check slot availability before quoting.
