# Maintenance Manual: Gas-Insulated Switchgear

## SF6 gas monitoring

The SF6 gas density monitor triggers a low-pressure alarm at 0.52 MPa absolute. The lockout of the circuit breaker is triggered at 0.50 MPa absolute. After an alarm, refill the compartment and record the amount of gas that was added.

## Leak rate

The leak rate of each gas compartment must stay below 0.5 percent per year. Compartments above this value must be inspected for leaks within 30 days.

## Inspection

Carry out a visual inspection of all gas compartments and the operating mechanism once per year.
