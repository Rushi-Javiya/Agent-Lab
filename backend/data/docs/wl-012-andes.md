# Win/Loss Report: Andes Transmission

Andes Transmission, a transmission company in South America, signed a contract with us on 19 May 2026 for a 230 kV static var compensator. We won this deal against NordSwitch.

We offered EUR 5.3 million and NordSwitch offered EUR 5.45 million, so our price was EUR 150,000 lower. However, the customer told us that price was not the main reason. Andes Transmission has bought from us for more than ten years under a framework agreement, and its engineers trust our service team to react fast when something goes wrong.

The customer said that this trust and the existing frame agreement were the reasons to stay with us, even though a new supplier made an attractive offer.

Next steps: we will offer Andes Transmission an extension of the framework agreement for three more years.
