# Win/Loss Report: Helvetia Grid

Customer: Helvetia Grid
Region: Central Europe
Date: 2025-11-18
Outcome: Won
Our price: EUR 7,400,000
Competitor: NordSwitch
Competitor price: EUR 7,900,000

## Summary

Helvetia Grid tendered 170 kV gas-insulated switchgear for a new substation in Switzerland. The tender documents required a national grid certification for all primary equipment.

## Why we won

We were the only bidder that already held the Swiss grid certification. NordSwitch planned to obtain it during the project, which the customer rated as a schedule risk. Our price of EUR 7,400,000 was also EUR 500,000 below the NordSwitch bid of EUR 7,900,000.

## Lessons

Certifications are a strong differentiator in regulated markets. We will keep the certification matrix for key countries up to date.
