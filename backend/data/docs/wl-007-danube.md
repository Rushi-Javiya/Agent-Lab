# Win/Loss Report: Danube Power

Customer: Danube Power
Region: Eastern Europe
Date: 2025-10-03
Outcome: Lost
Our price: EUR 2,400,000
Competitor: GridCore
Competitor price: EUR 2,150,000

## Summary

Danube Power tendered a protection and control system for three substations in Romania. The customer wanted a standard solution without customisation.

## Why we lost

GridCore offered a standard package for EUR 2,150,000. Our offer of EUR 2,400,000 included a customised engineering study that the customer did not value. The price gap of EUR 250,000 decided the tender.

## Lessons

For standard protection packages, we should lead with a catalogue solution and offer engineering studies as an option.
