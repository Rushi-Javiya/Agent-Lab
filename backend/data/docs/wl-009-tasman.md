# Win/Loss Report: Tasman Networks

In January 2026 the Asia Pacific grid operator Tasman Networks closed a tender for two 132 kV power transformers, and the contract was awarded on 22 January 2026 to VoltEdge. We lost the deal.

We had offered EUR 3.9 million for the two units. VoltEdge offered EUR 3.6 million and promised delivery 10 weeks faster than we could, because they had transformers in stock at a regional warehouse.

The Tasman project team told us that the schedule mattered more than the price gap. Their outage window on the grid was fixed, and a late delivery would have forced them to wait a full year for the next window. The faster delivery from VoltEdge was therefore the reason for the decision, not the lower price.

Next steps: sales and production planning should agree on a stock strategy for 132 kV transformers in the Asia Pacific region.
